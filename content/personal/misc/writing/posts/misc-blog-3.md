## A long overdue return.

After a year of lots of stuff going on and some life changing
events, recovery was needed. Things then started to get busy —
between my job, relationships, healing and more, the site got
delayed. Getting restarted was getting harder and harder with
each passing day.

## The current state of things

Since that initial creation I’ve had a change of heart and mind.
While this is still a key goal, I also want to put more personal
posts out too; reviews, life updates, anything really. This is
now a journal as well as a learning journey — and that should
make it easier to publish even when I feel like I’ve got nothing
“big to show.

## Some future plans

Over time I’d like to implement more Node.js, some Ruby on
Rails, and maybe React or Angular too. I may return to R with
RStudio, or do some PHPMyAdmin/QGIS for geo-spatial web again —
I always enjoyed data visualisation. Expect more misc posts and
reviews too.
