This page is a simple ghost reference. I keep evidence notes, then add
personal "tells" as I learn them.

## Evidence types

- EMF Level 5
- Ultraviolet
- Freezing Temperatures
- Ghost Orb
- Ghost Writing
- Spirit Box
- D.O.T.S Projector

On some difficulties, not all evidence will be available, so
learning behaviour becomes important.

## Quick tells I look for

- How fast it is during a hunt (and whether it ramps up with line of
  sight).
- How often it throws items, and how violent the throws feel.
- Whether it has any unique hunt visuals (visibility changes, model
  swaps, etc).
