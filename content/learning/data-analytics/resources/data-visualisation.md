## Learning notes

Chart structure, colour discipline, and storytelling with data.

## Milestones

- Choose the right chart for the question
- Titles + labels that tell the story
- Consistency (scales, units, formats)
- Colour as emphasis, not decoration
- Accessibility (contrast + not colour-only)
