## V5.1 — the personal-site refresh

- Quieter purple and green styling, compact menus and topic lists.
- Learning posts use an author column, post details, a contents list and a signature.
- The journal uses a book-like reading face; anime, games and investigation notes have their own layouts.
- Articles, watch-list entries and reference notes are readable without waiting for JavaScript.
- Themes only change colours; they no longer rebuild the page.
- Search, filters and copy buttons enhance the static pages.
- The site builds from Markdown, templates and plain CSS/JavaScript with one Python command.
- Old article addresses redirect to their current pages.
- Damaged Steam charts and the favicon were recovered from the original source upload.
- The repository includes editing, preview, update and recovery instructions.

Content dates and saved hobby data are retained from the source archive unless a specific correction is recorded. Old snapshots are not presented as live data.
